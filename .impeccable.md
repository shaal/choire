## Design Context

### Users
The user requested a working choir music tool that generates singing from notes and lyrics. They chose a composer's studio: a precise piano roll and a restrained dark interface. The primary workflow is composing and auditioning four vocal parts, adjusting the ensemble, and exporting audio.

### Brand Personality
Precise, composed, tactile. A dependable musical instrument, with the score as the central surface. Product name: Choire.

### Aesthetic Direction
A restrained dark desktop studio with warm neutral surfaces, sand accents, thin dividers, clear typography, and distinct muted colors for soprano, alto, tenor, and bass. Avoid marketing-page structure, neon decoration, and ornamental metrics.

### Design Principles
- Notes and lyrics occupy the largest workspace.
- Every audio control changes rendered sound; distinguish edited scores from current audio.
- Make note editing precise with snap, keyboard controls, and numeric fields.
- Keep rendering progress, errors, and model readiness visible.
- Keep useful mobile access through scrolling and stacked panels, with accessible labels and reduced motion.
